# `Game` fields grouped into `ServerState` and `ClientState`

Stage A of the gameplay client/server split (#2210) groups server-only fields of `sunrise_client::game::Game<'a>` (143 fields) into a trailing `server: ServerState` field (`sunrise_client::game::server_state::ServerState`) and client-only fields (266 fields) into a trailing `client: ClientState` field (`sunrise_client::game::client_state::ClientState`). The remaining 196 fields stay directly on `Game<'a>` (136 shared/mode fields plus 60 fields kept on `Game<'a>` to preserve Rust field drop order).

The stable `mod_api` surface and published loops are unchanged. Native mods that reach `Game` internals through `sunrise-internals`, `[[native.mixin]]`, `[[native.loop]]`, or `[[native.bridge]]` see 409 `internal:field:sunrise_client::game::Game<'_>::<field>` entries move to `ServerState` or `ClientState` (with `Game<'_>::server` and `Game<'_>::client` added; all changes are advisory in `internals.json`).

Migrate native mod helpers and branches by updating moved field accesses from `game.<field>` to `game.server.<field>` or `game.client.<field>` (the authoritative mapping is in `tools/game_field_owners.tsv`), updating any `[[native.bridge]]` on a moved field to target `sunrise_client::game::server_state::ServerState` or `sunrise_client::game::client_state::ClientState`, and running `cargo sunrise-mod internals sync`. `python3 tools/game_field_owners.py --fix <paths>` is provided as a migration aid that normalises accesses in both directions and reports ambiguous receivers for manual review; always review its diff before committing.
